<objective>
Migrate every remaining direct WhatsApp/PDF send (follow-ups, monthly report, and the
dashboard's order-status endpoint) onto the durable outbox (`outbox_jobs` +
`process_outbox_jobs()`), which is already the single send path for the bot's own message
pipeline (`app/services/processor.py`'s `handle_message()`). This is the first half of Phase
4 decision 1 ("delete retry_queue.py, outbox becomes the single retry mechanism") — it builds
the outbox-based replacement for every call site retry_queue used to catch failures for,
including introducing a new `pdf_invoice` outbox job kind. Plan 04-04 deletes the now-unused
retry_queue.py/retry_actions.py afterward, once this replacement exists and is tested.

Purpose: no customer-facing send or generated document may bypass the durable outbox — a
WhatsApp API outage must delay delivery, never silently drop it.
Output: `queue_pdf_invoice()` + a `pdf_invoice` job kind in `processor.py`; `followup.py`,
`monthly_report.py`, and `ui_api.py`'s status endpoint all enqueueing instead of sending inline.
</objective>
