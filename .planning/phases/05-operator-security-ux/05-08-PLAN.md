<objective>
Make failures and collisions legible to a non-technical operator.

Today `/alerts` shows `phone`, `attempts: 2/3`, `kind: whatsapp_message` and a raw stack string.
The aunt needs "تحتاج انتباهك الآن — رسالة لم تصل إلى فاطمة، تابعي المحادثة معها". Plan 05-06
already moved that framing server-side; this plan renders it.

The same plan also added a 409 conflict protocol to the status-change endpoint. This plan builds
the dialog that lets her pick the winner when she and the bot both act on one customer, instead
of a silent last-write-wins.

Purpose: success criterion 4 (see and recover from failed communications) and the bot-vs-aunt
conflict decision from success criterion 2.
Output: a reworked alerts page and a conflict dialog on the orders page.
</objective>
